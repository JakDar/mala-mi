# Mała Mi i Papryczkowa Wieża 🌶️

A 3D browser platformer: Little My climbs the endless Moomin-house tower (Icy Tower style),
collects chili peppers and escapes the Groke (Buka), who freezes everything from below.
Now and then Moominmamma bursts out of a window of the house and hops after her with a plate of pancakes
(charging in long leaps when she falls behind) — if she catches Mi, all power-ups
are gone and a well-fed (rounder, slower) Mi has to digest for a while.

**Play:** https://jakdar.github.io/mala-mi/

Single self-contained `index.html` (Three.js from CDN). Desktop: arrows/WASD + Space.
Mobile: tilt the phone to run, tap anywhere to jump (or switch to the touch joystick).

Debug URL flags: `?debug` (screenshots + `__tick` stepping), `?closeup=angle,dist`, `?touch`, `?autostart`.
